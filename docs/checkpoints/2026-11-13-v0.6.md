## Objective and reason

v0.5's cutout character looked geometric/archaic despite a working motion/render pipeline. The accepted upgrade changes character image semantics: `pose_atlas` displays **complete transparent illustrated full-body poses** instead of assembling body parts out of polygon drawings and geometric skeleton sprites.

## Working architecture

- `src/aetherpaper/pose_atlas.py`: deterministic illustrated pose selection, scene-relative safe PNG paths, cached image decoding with file modification awareness, constant painted foot baselines, mirror-aware contact position, hand anchor lookup and reliable RGBA compositing.
- `aetherpaper posepack`: transparent grid layout splitting, alpha validation, per-cell safety, checksum receipt, idempotent import, legacy receipt migration, no unsafe overwrite of edited art.
- `aetherpaper pose-demo`: backward-compatible regression demo using v0.5 source assets; its output must not be confused with genuine art enhancement.
- `visual.type=pose_atlas` schema, director `sprite_state` interpolation keyframes, asset manifests and physical prop contact from the painted hand.
- `src/aetherpaper/pose_demo.py` + `tests/test_pose_atlas_v06.py` and `tests/test_ai_courier_v06.py`.
- `src/aetherpaper/paint.py`, `model.py`, `timeline.py`, `choreography.py`, `cli.py`, `assets.py` updated for v0.6.
- Dedicated source and QA publication workflow `.github/workflows/v06-illustration-release.yml`.

## Visual and technical defects remaining

The person is clearly more visually sophisticated than the v0.5 geometric character, with painted face, scarf, windblown hair, rich coat and boots. The scenery is still stylized flat shapes, and the motion jumps between six source full-body drawings. It does **not** have photorealistic acting, authentic turns, rich intermediate frames, automatic anatomy extraction, mesh skinning, or true audio-driven lip sync. More pose frames and hand-art-directed backgrounds are the next quality bottlenecks.

Do not merge into main automatically. Keep the PR stacked against v0.5 until visual review and acceptance by the user.
